description=Sync a QGIS project to the Mappa Mundi field app over local Wi-Fi.
about=Runs a small sync server on this computer. The Mappa Mundi phone app downloads layers, styles and offline raster tiles, and pushes field edits and photos back into the project's layers. Phones pair by scanning a QR code; no cloud account is needed. Requires the Mappa Mundi app on an Android phone on the same network.

changelog=0.3.1 - Pairing-code alphabet built from standard characters (no false secret-scan alarm).
    0.3.0 - Sync is encrypted (AES-GCM) with a pairing code that replaces the PIN. Needs Mappa Mundi app 1.2.0 or newer: re-pair phones by scanning the new QR code.
    0.2.10 - Offline raster tiles keep one colour stretch (what QGIS shows when you build them), so styles that recalculate min/max per view no longer leave seams between blocks of tiles.
    0.2.9 - Offline raster tiles are drawn only inside the field area (edge tiles transparent), instead of filling whole tiles around it.
    0.2.8 - Fixes a crash with categorized styles that have an "all other values" class. Phones only get the features inside the field area, and layers too big for a phone (over 50,000 features) are left out instead of exhausting memory. The panel scrolls instead of stretching the QGIS window, and the QR code size can be changed or enlarged.
    0.2.7 - Field area: share only the layers with data in an area (map view, drawn rectangle or selected features) or on screen, and clip offline raster tiles to the area.
    0.2.6 - Links to get the Mappa Mundi Android app while it is in testing.
    0.2.5 - New contact email.
    0.2.4 - Author shown as Epiwssa.
    0.2.3 - New icon matching the Mappa Mundi app.
    0.2.2 - Qt6 ready: fully scoped Qt and QGIS enums.
    0.2.1 - Security scan clean-up: no silent error handling, MD5 marked as non-security, documented LAN listening.
    0.2.0 - Survey-grade GPS attributes (gnss_* fields added automatically), layers created on phones are added to the project, renamed to Mappa Mundi Sync.
    0.1.0 - First release: Wi-Fi sync, QR pairing, offline raster tiles, photo upload.
