# Join the Mappa Mundi test

Mappa Mundi, the Android field app for QGIS, is in testing on Google Play. Anyone can join. It's free, and you can leave at any time.

Do these steps **on your Android phone**, signed in with the Google account (Gmail) you use for the Play Store.

1. **Join the tester group:** open https://groups.google.com/g/mappa-mundi-testers and tap **Join group**.

   If it says **Ask to join group**, send the request. Telling us what you map and which phone you have helps. We approve requests by hand, so it may take a day.
2. **Become a tester:** once you're in the group, open https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.epiwssa.mappamundi and tap **Become a tester**.
3. **Install:** tap **Download it on Google Play**, or search the Play Store for **Mappa Mundi** and install it.

If step 2 says the app isn't available, step 1 hasn't gone through yet. Wait a few minutes, or until your request is approved, and try again. Make sure the Play Store is using the same Google account (tap your picture, top right).

## Then connect it to QGIS

1. In QGIS, install **Mappa Mundi Sync**: go to *Plugins → Manage and Install Plugins*, and on the *Settings* tab tick *Show also experimental plugins*.
2. Open a project, click the Mappa Mundi toolbar button and press **Start sync server**.
3. In the app, tap **Connect to QGIS → Scan QR code**. The phone and computer must be on the same Wi-Fi.

Keep the app and the plugin up to date together: sync between them is encrypted, and new versions of one may need the new version of the other.

## Feedback

Problems, ideas or questions: mrepiwssa@gmail.com, or open an issue at https://github.com/Epiwssa/mappa-mundi-sync/issues.

To leave the test, open the step 2 link and tap **Leave the program**, then leave the group.
